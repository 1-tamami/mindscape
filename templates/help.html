Frequently Asked Questions

General Questions

How do I answer questions?

Simply type your thoughts in the text area below each question and click the Send button. You can choose to make your answer public or keep it private using the privacy toggle.

How can I skip a question?

Click the refresh/skip button next to the answer box, or click the "Mindscape" logo in the navigation bar to get a new random question.

What's the difference between public and private answers?

Public answers are visible to all users in the "All Users" tab and can be viewed by clicking on them. Private answers are only visible to you in your "Your Logs" tab.

Can I edit or delete my answers?

Yes! In your "Your Logs" tab, you can edit any answer by clicking the pencil icon, or delete it by clicking the trash icon. You can also change the privacy setting when editing.

How can I filter and sort my answers?

Use the dropdown menus above your answer list to filter by category (like Personal Growth, Relationships), depth (Surface, Deep), or sort by date (Latest or Oldest first).

I forgot my password. How can I reset it?

Click "Forgot your password?" on the login page, enter your email address, and we'll send you a temporary password. After logging in, you'll be prompted to change it to a permanent password.

How can I report inappropriate content?

In the "All Users" tab, click the three dots (⋯) next to any answer and select "Report". Choose a reason and provide additional details if needed. We review all reports promptly.

Is my data private and secure?

Yes! Private answers are only visible to you. Public answers show your username but no other personal information. We don't share your data with third parties. See our Terms of Use for details.
