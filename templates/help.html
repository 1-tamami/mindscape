Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to the questions we hear most often. If you need something more specific, please drop us a message.

Getting started

How do I answer questions?
Type your thoughts in the answer field and select Send. Use the privacy toggle to decide whether the post stays private or is shared publicly.
How can I skip a question?
Click the refresh icon next to the answer area. You can also click the memochou wordmark in the top-left corner to load a new random question.
What's the difference between public and private answers?
Public answers appear in the “All Users” feed with your username. Private answers are visible only to you in “Your Logs”. You can change the privacy setting later when editing an entry.

Managing entries

Can I edit or delete previous reflections?
Yes. Open “Your Logs”, select an entry, and choose the edit or delete option. Edits let you revise the text and adjust the public/private setting.
How do filters work on the My Page view?
Use the category, depth, and time filters to narrow the list of answers you see. It’s a quick way to review specific themes or track how you felt during a particular period.
Can I download my data?
Yes. Visit your account settings and choose the export option to download a CSV file of your entries.

Community & safety

How can I report inappropriate content?
In the “All Users” tab, open the menu (⋯) for any public post and select Report. Choose a reason and add context if needed—we review every report.
Is my data private and secure?
Private responses stay visible only to you. Public posts show your username but no personal details. We never sell or share your data. Review the Terms of Use for more information.

Still puzzled by something? Send us a note and we’ll help as soon as we can.
