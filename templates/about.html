About memochou

memochou is a quiet space for daily self-reflection. We designed it for people who want to explore their own thoughts without the noise of debates or social feeds.

What you can do here

  • Capture personal reflections through thoughtful daily questions.
  • Keep your answers private or share them publicly—it's always your choice.
  • Review your past entries to notice patterns and growth over time.
  • Download your data whenever you want a copy of your writing.

How it works

  1. Create a free account.
  2. Answer the daily question or skip until something resonates.
  3. Choose whether to keep your response private or share it.
  4. Return anytime to continue your personal record.

If you'd like to begin, create an account or sign in to pick up where you left off.
